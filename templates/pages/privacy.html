Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

What we collect

When you create a Future Store account, we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password — we never store passwords in plain text. When you place an order, we also collect the order details and shipping information you provide at checkout.

How we use it

We use your information to operate your account, process and ship orders, respond to support requests, and show you your own order history and reviews. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

We use a small number of essential cookies to keep you signed in and remember your cart between visits. See our Cookie Policy for the full list.

Data retention

We keep your account and order history for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting support.

Your choices

You can review and update your profile information from your Dashboard at any time, or contact us to request a copy or deletion of your data.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out through our Contact page.
