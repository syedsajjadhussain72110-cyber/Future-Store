Help Center

How can we help?

Answers to the questions we hear most. Can't find what you need? Contact us or use the AI Assistant.

Orders & Shipping

How do I track my order?

Go to My Orders while signed in to see the status of every order you've placed, from Confirmed through Delivered.

How long does shipping take?

Most orders arrive within 3-5 business days. Shipping is free on orders over $50.

Can I change or cancel an order after placing it?

Contact us as soon as possible after placing your order. If it hasn't shipped yet, we can usually update or cancel it.

Returns & Refunds

What's your return policy?

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, provided they're unused and in their original packaging. See our full Returns page for details.

When will I get my refund?

Refunds are issued to your original payment method once we receive and inspect the returned item, typically within 5-7 business days.

Account & Orders

Do I need an account to shop?

You can browse and search freely without an account, but you'll need to create one to add items to your cart and check out.

How do I leave a product review?

Open any product you've purchased and scroll to the Customer Reviews section — you can leave one rating and comment per product while signed in.

Payment

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on Delivery, Credit/Debit Card, and EasyPaisa/JazzCash are all available at checkout.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. All transactions are encrypted, and we never store your full card details on our servers.
