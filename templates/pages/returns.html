Returns & Refunds

Easy, 30-day returns

We want you to love what you ordered. If something isn't right, here's exactly how to send it back.

1

Start your return

Go to My Orders, find the item, and select "Write a Review" or contact us with your order number to begin.

2

Pack it up

Place the item back in its original packaging with all accessories, manuals, and tags attached.

3

Ship it back

Use the prepaid return label we email you, or drop it at any partner carrier location.

4

Get refunded

Once we receive and inspect your return, your refund is issued to your original payment method within 5-7 business days.

Return policy details

  • Items must be returned within 30 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unused, undamaged, and in original packaging.
  • Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items.
  • Perishable goods (Grocery category) and personal care items cannot be returned once opened.
  • Gift cards and digital products are final sale.
